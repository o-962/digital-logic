The 10 golden rules of Chapter 1

  1. Value = Σ digit × baseposition. Positions go …2, 1, 0 . −1, −2…
  2. Digits of base r are 0 … r−1. The digit "r" never appears in base r, so (128)8 is invalid.
  3. Decimal → base r: integer part divide by r and read remainders bottom-up. Fraction part multiply by r and read integers top-down.
  4. 2 ↔ 8: group 3 bits. 2 ↔ 16: group 4 bits. Group outward from the point and pad with zeros at the far ends.
  5. 8 ↔ 16: go through binary. In general, if b = ak, group k digits (3 ↔ 9 groups 2, 4 ↔ 16 groups 2, 2 ↔ 32 groups 5).
  6. 1's complement = flip every bit. 2's complement = 1's + 1, or keep bits up to and including the first 1 from the right and flip the rest.
  7. n-bit 2's complement range: −2n−1 … +2n−1−1. Signed-magnitude and 1's complement: −(2n−1−1) … +(2n−1−1), and they have two zeros.
  8. Subtraction with 2's complement: add and discard the end carry. With 1's complement: add the end carry back in (end-around carry). No carry means the result is negative and in complement form.
  9. BCD: each decimal digit gets 4 bits. In BCD addition, if a digit sum is > 9 or produces a carry, add 6 (0110).
  10. Bits needed for M items: n = ⌈log2 M⌉. Gray code changes exactly one bit per step. Parity detects single-bit (odd count) errors.
